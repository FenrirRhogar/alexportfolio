Meridian roasts six days a week out of a converted garage, and their old packaging looked like it — in the bad way. The brief was one sentence: "make it feel like the coffee tastes."

The system is built on a single gradient that shifts hue by roast profile, screen-printed by hand in small runs so no two boxes are quite identical. The wordmark borrows its geometry from the roaster's drum window.

**Scope:** Naming consultation, identity, bag and box packaging, signage for the roastery counter.
