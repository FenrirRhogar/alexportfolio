Field Notes came to me with a stack of disposable cameras and a rough idea: a quarterly journal for people who hike further than the trailhead sign recommends. The identity had to survive both a glossy cover and a mud-splattered field copy.

I built the mark as a stamp first, a logotype second — something that felt more like a surveyor's tool than a startup wordmark. The palette pulls three colors directly from contour-line topographic maps, and the typography pairs a no-nonsense grotesk with a single, slightly feral display serif for pull-quotes.

**Scope:** Logotype, print system, packaging for the annual box set, and a restrained set of social templates that resisted the urge to be templates at all.
