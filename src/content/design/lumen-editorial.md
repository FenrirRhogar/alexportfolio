Lumen is a photography quarterly with a print run small enough that every decision could be a little reckless. I built a modular grid loose enough to let full-bleed spreads and dense caption columns coexist without ever feeling like two different magazines.

Typesetting leans on a single serif at three optical sizes, with captions set in a mono for a lab-notebook feel that nods to the contributors' darkroom backgrounds.

**Scope:** Grid system, typesetting, cover direction for issues 04–07.
